# Privacy Notice — Mobile Draft for Review

BletëFjalë Mobile is designed for local-first terminology translation. Typed text, custom glossary data, and OCR text are processed on the device. OCR is performed by the installed ML Kit component. The app does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, or cloud sync in this version.

Before paid public release, have this notice reviewed for your business entity, actual data flows, support channel, payment provider, and target countries. Update store Data Safety / privacy declarations before submission if you add analytics, subscriptions, cloud backup, online translation, crash reporting, or account features.

# Privacy Notice — Draft for review

**Last updated:** August 2026

BletëFjalë is designed around local terminology translation. Text typed into the app, custom glossary entries, and imported files are processed on the user's device. Custom glossary data is stored in the local application storage unless the user explicitly exports it.

The image OCR component may retrieve its language model during first-time setup when an internet connection is available. The app does not deliberately upload the user's image or document contents for glossary translation.

Before commercial release, replace this draft with a privacy notice reviewed for your actual business, country of establishment, payment provider, analytics choices, support channel, and target markets. If you later add accounts, telemetry, cloud sync, advertising, or online machine translation, update the notice and the store data-safety declarations before release.
